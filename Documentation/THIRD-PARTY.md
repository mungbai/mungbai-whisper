# Third-party components

- OpenAI Whisper Large v3 Turbo weights, distributed on Hugging Face under the
  MIT license.
- MLX and MLX Whisper, used for Apple Silicon inference under their respective
  MIT licenses.
- FluidAudio, used for local Core ML speaker diarization under the Apache 2.0
  license.
- FFmpeg, used to decode and normalize supported recording formats.

Model files are kept beneath `Library/Models`; project source is kept beneath
`Application/Source`.
